How it works.

Four steps. You only pay once you say yes.

  1. Step 01

    We build your site first, free.

    A custom preview made from your public business info. It is ready before we reach out.

  2. Step 02

    You look at it.

    Tell us what to change. Text, services, photos: point it out and we fix it.

  3. Step 03

    Say yes.

    Sign up online in a few minutes. Payment is by card.

  4. Step 04

    We go live.

    We connect your domain, your existing one or a new one we register for you, and keep the site running.

The offer.

One plan. One price. No surprises.

$179 per month No setup fee
  • First month is charged at signup, then monthly.
  • 12-month minimum term, then month to month.
  • Cancel by replying to any of our emails. It takes effect at the end of the paid month.
  • If you cancel within the first 12 months, the rest of the minimum term is still owed.

Full terms are shown before you sign up.

What you get.

Everything a roofing site needs to stay online and bring in leads.

Included

In your $179.

  • A custom website for your business.
  • Hosting.
  • SSL certificate.
  • Up to 2 content edits per month, done within 3 business days. One edit is one change request for text or photos. Unused edits do not roll over.
  • Ongoing basic SEO upkeep.
  • A lead form on your site, with leads going to you.
Not included

What we leave alone.

  • Email or email hosting.
  • DNS work beyond the @ and www records we send you.
  • Registrar support.
  • Larger redesigns or new features. These are quoted separately.

We never touch your email.

What we need from you.

  • Your approval of the preview. Nothing goes live until you say so.
  • Photos of your own work, if you want them on the site. You confirm you own them.
  • Any changes to text or services. Tell us what is wrong or missing.

Questions.

Short answers. Email us if yours is not here.

Do I own my domain?
A domain you already own stays yours. If we register one for you, it is held in our account while the service is active. When the service ends, we transfer it to you free, once your account is paid in full.
What if I want changes?
Reply to any of our emails. You get up to 2 content edits per month, done within 3 business days. Larger redesigns or new features are quoted separately.
Is there a contract?
Yes. There is a 12-month minimum term, then it goes month to month. Full terms are shown before you sign up.
What happens if I cancel?
Reply to any of our emails. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid month. Within the first 12 months, the rest of the minimum term is still owed. When the service ends, we take the site down.
Do you guarantee rankings or leads?
No. We work hard to keep your site fast and online, but we make no guarantees on rankings, leads or uptime beyond reasonable effort.
How do I pay?
By card, through Stripe. We never see your card number.
Is there a setup fee?
No. You pay $179 per month, starting with the first month at signup.
Who am I working with?
Dutch Mike, run by Michael Melchiot, based in Philadelphia, PA.
